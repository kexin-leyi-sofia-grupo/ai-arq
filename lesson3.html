Section 02

RGEU — General Urban Building Regulations (Housing)

Habitability, spatial, lighting, ventilation, and sanitary standards for residential buildings under the Regulamento Geral das Edificações Urbanas (Decreto-Lei n.º 38 382/1951, consolidated version).

Item Article Value or Criterion (SI) Literal Citation (max 30 words) Condition to Respect in Project Consultation Date
1. Minimum free ceiling height and floor-to-floor height in housing RGEU, Art. 65.º, n.º 1 and n.º 2 Minimum floor-to-floor height: 2.70 m. Minimum free ceiling height: 2.40 m. Exceptions (service/circulation): 2.20 m in vestibules, corridors, sanitary facilities, pantries, and storage rooms. "A altura mínima, piso a piso, em edificações destinadas à habitação é de 2,70m [...], não podendo ser o pé-direito livre mínimo inferior a 2,40 m." Legal requirement: ensure a free ceiling height of 2.40 m in habitable rooms and a 2.70 m floor-to-floor dimension between finished floors, allowing reduction to 2.20 m exclusively in technical, sanitary, and internal circulation areas. 26/10/2026
2. Minimum areas and dimensions of residential rooms RGEU, Art. 66.º, n.º 1; Art. 67.º, n.º 1; Art. 69.º, n.º 1 Double bedroom: 10.5 m²; Twin bedroom: 9 m²; Single bedroom: 6.5 m². Living room: 10 m² (T0/T1), 12 m² (T2), 16 m² (T3–T6). Kitchen: 6 m². Minimum gross area (Ab): T0 (35 m²), T1 (52 m²), T2 (72 m²), T3 (91 m²), T4 (105 m²). Minimum linear dimension: 2.10 m (if area < 9.5 m²); circle of diameter ≥ 2.40 m (for area between 9.5 m² and 12 m²). "Os compartimentos de habitação não poderão ser em número e área inferiores aos indicados no quadro [...]. Quando a respectiva área for menor que 9,5 m², a dimensão mínima será 2,10 m" Legal requirement: dimension the usable area (Au) and habitable area (Ah) of each room respecting the minimums per typology (Tx) in the Table and ensuring the inscription of the regulatory manoeuvrability circles. 26/10/2026
3. Natural lighting openings (direct communication with the exterior) RGEU, Art. 71.º, n.º 1 Total area of openings: not less than 1/10 (10%) of the room's usable area. Minimum absolute area: 1.08 m² (measured on the rough opening). "Os compartimentos das habitações [...] serão sempre iluminados e ventilados por um ou mais vãos [...] cuja área total não será inferior a um décimo da área do compartimento com o mínimo de 1,08 m²" Legal requirement: provide windows or openings in direct communication with the exterior in all habitable rooms, with a useful glazed area of at least 10% of the room's floor surface. 26/10/2026
4. Ventilation conditions of spaces RGEU, Art. 71.º, n.º 1; Art. 72.º; Art. 87.º, n.º 1 and 2; Art. 109.º General ventilation: cross ventilation generally ensured by windows on two opposite façades. Sanitary facilities: external opening with total area ≥ 0.54 m² and openable part ≥ 0.36 m² on the rough opening. If unfeasible, constant renewal by forced/mechanical ventilation or duct. Kitchens: devices for evacuation of fumes and gases (private ducts/chimneys). "Deverá ficar assegurada a ventilação transversal do conjunto de cada habitação, em regra por meio de janelas dispostas em duas fachadas opostas." Legal requirement: design the orientation of dwellings to allow cross air flow and ensure specific mechanical exhaust systems or ducts in sanitary facilities without direct lighting and in kitchens. 26/10/2026
5. Yards, setbacks, and salubrity conditions RGEU, Art. 59.º; Art. 60.º; Art. 62.º, § 2.º; Art. 73.º; Art. 76.º Setback between opposite façades: ≥ 10 m (if with housing openings). Yard depth: ≥ half the height of the adjacent rear façade, with a minimum of 6 m and free uncovered area ≥ 40 m². Front setback of windows: ≥ half the height of the opposite wall/façade, minimum 3 m and 2 m free width on each side of the axis. Wall protection: impermeable strip along the building with width ≥ 1 m. "[...] a distância mínima entre fachadas de edificações nas quais existam vãos de compartimentos de habitação não poderá ser inferior a 10 metros." Legal requirement: ensure a minimum 10 m setback from opposing façades with windows, guarantee the depth and minimum area of the private yard, and provide the impermeable protection strip on the adjacent ground. 26/10/2026

Accessibility Standards — Decreto-Lei n.º 163/2006

Technical accessibility conditions for buildings, public establishments, public roads, and residential buildings under the Regime da Acessibilidade and its Annex of Technical Standards.

Item Reference (Article / Section) Value or Criterion (SI) Literal Citation (max 30 words) Condition to Respect in Project Consultation Date
1. Accessible route (public road, main entrance, and all spaces) DL 163/2006, Art. 2.º, n.º 3; Annex, Sections 1.1, 1.2.1, 2.1.1, and 3.2.1 Sidewalks on public road: free width ≥ 1.50 m (continuous channel ≥ 1.20 m). Free height: ≥ 2.40 m (enclosed spaces) / ≥ 2.00 m (interiors). Entrance & lobby: 360° rotation (diameter ≥ 1.50 m). 2-storey residential building: initial vertical mechanical means waived. "Os edifícios e estabelecimentos devem ser dotados de pelo menos um percurso, designado de acessível, que proporcione o acesso seguro e confortável das pessoas com mobilidade condicionada..." Internal doors and dwellings: useful width ≥ 0.77 m and useful height ≥ 2.00 m. Floor thresholds: maximum height ≤ 0.02 m. "As portas de entrada/saída dos edifícios e estabelecimentos devem ter um largura útil não inferior a 0,87 m, medida entre a face da folha da porta quando aberta..." Legal requirement: design the exterior access door with a minimum free useful opening of 0.87 m and internal/dwelling doors with at least 0.77 m, eliminating thresholds above 2 cm. 26/10/2026
3. Circulations (landings, galleries, corridors) and manoeuvring zones DL 163/2006, Annex, Sections 2.3.1, 2.3.3, 3.3.2, and 4.4.1 Common corridors/landings: width ≥ 1.20 m. "Os patamares, galerias e corredores devem possuir uma largura não inferior a 1,2 m." and "Rotação de 360°: A ≥ 1,50 m" Legal requirement: maintain widths of 1.20 m in common areas and 1.10 m in the dwelling, ensuring 1.50 m manoeuvring zones at strategic locations and every 10 m of corridor. 26/10/2026
4. Handrails DL 163/2006, Annex, Sections 2.3.4, 2.4.9, 2.5.8, 2.5.9, and 4.11.1 Grip: diameter/width between 0.035 m and 0.05 m; wall clearance ≥ 0.035 m. Height: 0.85 m to 0.90 m on stairs/corridors. Ramps: double (0.70–0.75 m and 0.90–0.95 m) if inclination > 6%. Extension: ≥ 0.30 m at ends. "Os corrimãos e as barras de apoio devem ter um diâmetro ou largura das superfícies de preensão compreendido entre 0,035 m e 0,05 m..." Vertical access (lift, ramp, or platform) DL 163/2006, Annex, Sections 2.5.1, 2.6.2, 3.2.1, and 3.2.2, n.º 1 Ramps: inclination ≤ 6% (level difference ≤ 0.60 m, length ≤ 10 m) or ≤ 8% (level difference ≤ 0.40 m, length ≤ 5 m); width ≥ 1.20 m. 2-storey residential building: initial installation of lift/mechanical means waived. Design provision: provide for future installation of a stair-lifting platform. "Nos edifícios de habitação com um número de pisos sobrepostos inferior a cinco [...] podem não ser instalados meios mecânicos de comunicação vertical alternativos às escadas..." Adapted sanitary facilities DL 163/2006, Annex, Sections 2.9.4, 2.9.13, 2.9.19, and 3.3.4 Per dwelling: at least 1 adaptable sanitary facility with washbasin, toilet, bidet, and bathtub (or 0.80 m × 0.80 m shower tray with reservation for bathtub). Heights: toilet seat at 0.45 m (± 0.01 m); washbasin at 0.80 m (± 0.02 m). Rotation: 360° (diameter ≥ 1.50 m). "Em cada habitação deve existir pelo menos uma instalação sanitária que satisfaça [...] a disposição dos aparelhos sanitários [...] permitir a colocação de barras de apoio..." Legal requirement: design the main bathroom of the dwelling ensuring the internal 1.50 m diameter manoeuvring area, lateral transfer space at the toilet, and walls with resistance for future support bars. 26/10/2026
7. Reserved parking spaces DL 163/2006, Annex, Sections 2.8.2, 2.8.3, and 3.2.6 Housing: capacity < 13 spaces (exemption from reservation); 13 to 49 spaces (minimum 1 supplementary space in common area). Dimensions per space: useful width ≥ 2.50 m; lateral strip ≥ 1.00 m (may be shared); length ≥ 5.00 m. Signage: floor painting (≥ 1 m) and vertical sign. "Os lugares de estacionamento reservados devem: 1) Ter uma largura útil não inferior a 2,5 m; 2) Possuir uma faixa de acesso lateral com uma largura útil não inferior a 1 m..." Legal requirement: if the private/common car park of the building has 13 or more spaces, dimension 1 supplementary space with a total width of 3.50 m (2.50 m + 1.00 m access) duly signed. 26/10/2026

Applicable exceptions and conditions for invocation DL 163/2006, Art. 10.º, n.º 1, 2, 4, and 5 Existing buildings: invocable if works are disproportionately difficult, financially unfeasible, or affect cultural/historical heritage. "[...] o cumprimento das normas técnicas de acessibilidade [...] não é exigível quando as obras necessárias à sua execução sejam desproporcionadamente difíceis, requeiram a aplicação de meios económico-financeiros desproporcionados..."
